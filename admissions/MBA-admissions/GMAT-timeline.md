# GMAT guide: format, fees, dates and a prep plan

As of **16 October 2026**. The current exam is the **GMAT Focus Edition**. The old
"classic" GMAT was retired in early 2024. Confirm fees and rules on [mba.com](https://www.mba.com) before booking.

## 1. When is the GMAT held?

**There are no fixed attempts and no "four windows a year".** The GMAT runs **year-round, almost
every day**, at Pearson VUE test centres (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru and others) and online
from home. You pick a date and slot and book it, usually 2-4 weeks ahead.

So there is no "current attempt" to catch. The deadlines that matter are the **school deadlines**:

| Your target deadline | Take the GMAT by |
|---|---|
| ISB YLP (rounds ~Nov 2026 - early 2027) | Nov 2026 - Jan 2027 |
| US Round 2 for Fall 2027 (5-15 Jan 2027) | **~20 Dec 2026** (scores reach schools in 3-5 business days) |
| Deferred MBA programmes (HBS 2+2, Yale Silver Scholars, etc., ~Feb-Apr 2027) | **Jan-Feb 2027** |
| Route B: apply Sep 2029 | Any time before ~Mar 2029 (scores are valid 5 years) |

Leave room for **one retake**: book the first attempt 6-8 weeks before your real deadline.

### Retake rules
- At least **16 days** between attempts.
- At most **5 attempts in any rolling 12 months**, **8 in a lifetime**.
- You choose which score to send. Schools generally take your highest.
- Scores are valid **5 years**.

## 3. The exam

| | Quantitative Reasoning | Verbal Reasoning | Data Insights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Questions | 21 | 23 | 20 |
| Time | 45 min | 45 min | 45 min |
| What's tested | Problem solving: arithmetic and algebra, word problems. **No geometry, no calculator** | Reading comprehension, critical reasoning (no sentence correction) | Data sufficiency, multi-source reasoning, table analysis, graphics interpretation, two-part analysis. **On-screen calculator allowed** |
| Section score | 60-90 | 60-90 | 60-90 |

- **Total: 2 h 15 min**, plus one optional 10-minute break.
- **Total score: 205-805** (always ends in 5). All three sections count equally.
- **Section order is your choice.**
- **Question review & edit:** you can bookmark, review, and change up to **3 answers per section**.
- **Adaptive:** questions get harder or easier based on your answers.
- Unofficial score on screen at the end; official score report in ~3-5 business days.
- **No AWA essay** in the Focus Edition.

## 4. A 12-week prep plan (Oct-Dec 2026, ~12-15 hrs a week alongside B.Tech)

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 0 | **Diagnostic:** free Official Practice Exam 1, untimed review afterwards |
| 1-3 | **Quant** foundations: number properties, algebra, word problems (rate/work, mixtures, percents), statistics. IITians lose marks to speed and careless errors, not concepts |
| 3-6 | **Verbal:** critical reasoning (assumptions, strengthen/weaken, inference) and a reading-comprehension passage every day. For most engineers, Verbal decides the score |
| 6-9 | **Data Insights:** data sufficiency logic, then table/graph/multi-source sets with the calculator. Practise not using the calculator when estimating is faster |
| 9-11 | Full timed mocks, one a week (Official Practice Exams 2-6). Keep an error log and redo every miss |
| 12 | Light review, sleep, take the exam |

**Material:** GMAT Official Guide (Focus Edition) plus the Quant, Verbal and DI review books; GMAC
Official Practice Exams; GMAT Club forum (free question bank and debriefs). Paid courses many
people use: Target Test Prep (Quant), e-GMAT (Verbal), GMAT Ninja / Manhattan Prep.

## 5. Booking checklist
1. Create an account on **mba.com**.
2. Pick test centre or online. Online needs a quiet room, webcam and a whiteboard.
3. ID: your **passport**. The name must match exactly.
4. Choose up to 5 schools to receive scores free on test day (you can skip and send later).
5. After the test: accept the score (or cancel it within 72 hours, which schools never see).
